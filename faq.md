## Is Ahhlo free?

Yes. Logging, sync with one other caregiver, schedules and Patterns are free.
Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds more caregivers, more custom
event types, and export.

## Is there a subscription?

No. Pro is bought once through the App Store or Google Play. Nothing renews,
so there's nothing to cancel. On a new phone, use **Restore purchases** while
signed in to the same store account.

## What does Pro include?

- Up to five caregivers on a baby's care team (free is you plus one)
- Up to 20 custom event types (free is one)
- Export to CSV or PDF

## Do I need an account?

You sign in with Apple or Google. That's what lets you sync and invite people
later. Your logs stay on your phone until you turn on sync or invite a
caregiver.

## Can my partner use it if they have a different phone?

Yes. Ahhlo runs on iPhone and Android, and both of you see the same log.

## How do I invite someone?

Open the baby's care team and create an invitation. The other person enters the
code in their app. A code works once and expires after 15 minutes.

## Does it work without signal?

Yes. You can log offline, and it syncs when you're back online.

## Can I track more than one baby?

Yes. Add each baby and switch between them. Each baby has its own care team.

## What can I log?

Sleep, nursing (left/right timer with pause), bottles (breast milk, formula or
mixed), pumping (per side), diapers and baths. Custom event types cover
anything else, like medicine or tummy time. Volumes can be in ml or fl oz.

## Can I import data from another baby tracker?

Not yet. If you're switching, export from your old app first and keep the file.
There's more on that in [Switching trackers]({{ '/switching/' | relative_url }}).

## Does Ahhlo use AI on my data?

No. Patterns and trends are worked out from the entries you logged, nothing
else. We don't train AI models on your data or share it with anyone who does.

## Who can see my baby's data?

Only you and the caregivers you invite. There are no ads and no third-party
analytics SDKs, and we don't sell data. See the
[Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

## Which languages does it support?

English, Chinese, Spanish, Malay, Indonesian and Filipino.

## How do I delete my account?

From inside the app, or by email. See
[Delete your account]({{ '/delete-account/' | relative_url }}).
