<p class="updated">Ahhlo — account and data deletion</p>

This page explains how to delete your Ahhlo account and the data associated
with it. Deletion is available to everyone, whether or not you still have the
app installed.

## Delete from within the app (fastest)

1. Open Ahhlo and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → My account**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately.

## Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email
**[support@bean-studio.com](mailto:support@bean-studio.com)** from the email
address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my account".
We verify the request against your account email and process it within 30
days (usually much sooner).

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- your account record and sign-in identity;
- baby profiles you own, and all their logged events and schedules;
- your membership in any care teams you had joined.

Babies owned by **another** account (where you were an invited caregiver) are
not deleted — only your access to them is removed.

## What is retained

- Purchase records required for tax and accounting compliance are retained by
  the app stores (Apple/Google) under their policies; we retain a minimal
  transaction reference as required by law.
- Residual copies in encrypted backups of our database are purged on the
  backup rotation cycle (up to 30 days).

For full details, see our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})
