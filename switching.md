A lot of parents find Ahhlo the same way. The tracker they've used since the
hospital starts asking for a monthly fee, and suddenly there's nowhere to log
the 3 a.m. feed. This page covers what moving over looks like.

## What usually happened

We hear a few versions of it:

- A free app added a subscription, and you can't add new entries until you pay.
- You shared one log with your partner, and now neither of you can write to it.
- The basics still work, but sharing or export moved behind a paywall.

Apps cost money to run, so none of this is strange. Ahhlo just pays for itself
differently: some parents buy Pro once, and that's it. There's no monthly plan.

## What you get for free

- Logging sleep, nursing (with a left/right timer you can pause), bottles,
  pumping, diapers and baths.
- Sync with one other caregiver. You log a feed, it shows up on their phone.
  iPhone and Android can share the same log.
- Schedules, and Patterns built from your own logs.
- One custom event type, for whatever else you track.

## What Pro adds

Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play.

- Up to five caregivers on a baby's care team
- Up to 20 custom event types
- Export to CSV or PDF

## Moving over

1. **Export from your old app before you delete it.** Some apps keep export
   open after a trial ends, some don't. Either way, get the file while you can
   and keep it somewhere safe.
2. **Install Ahhlo** and sign in with Apple or Google.
3. **Add your baby** and log from now on. If a feed or nap is already under
   way, tap **Already started?** on the timer and set the real start time.
4. **Fill in today if you want to.** Switch the log sheet to **Earlier** to
   add the feeds and diapers from this morning.
5. **Invite your partner** from the baby's care team. They type the code into
   the app on their phone. Codes expire after 15 minutes, so send it when
   they're ready.

## Can I bring my old history?

Not yet. Ahhlo can't read other apps' export files today, so your history
starts on the day you switch. Keep the export from your old app as your record.

If importing would make the difference for you, tell us at
[support@bean-studio.com](mailto:support@bean-studio.com). It's one of the
things we're weighing, and hearing from parents helps us decide.

## A note on privacy

Your logs stay on your phone until you turn on sync or invite someone. After
that they're stored under your account, and the database only lets you and the
caregivers you invite read them. No ads, no third-party analytics, and we don't
sell your data or train AI on it. The details are in the
[Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
